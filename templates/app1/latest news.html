1. New Three-Language Rule for CBSE Students

The CBSE has introduced a new three-language requirement for students in Classes 9 and 10 from the 2026–27 academic session. Students will study three languages, with at least two required to be Indian languages. The third language will be assessed at the school level rather than through the regular Class 10 board examination. The change is part of wider efforts to promote multilingual education and follows the direction of the National Education Policy.

2. National Teachers' Awards 2026

On Teachers' Day, September 5, 2026, President Droupadi Murmu presented the National Teachers' Awards 2026 to 48 school teachers from across India. The award recognises teachers who have made outstanding contributions to education and inspired students through innovative teaching methods. The event highlights the important role teachers play in improving the quality of school education and helping students develop their skills and confidence.

3. Technology and AI in Schools

Technology and Artificial Intelligence are becoming an important part of school education. In India, AI is increasingly being introduced into school curricula, with the government promoting AI and Computational Thinking to help students develop problem-solving, digital and critical-thinking skills.

4. School Holidays in September 2026

September 2026 includes several school holidays across different parts of India because of festivals and regional occasions such as Janmashtami and Ganesh Chaturthi. However, holiday dates differ between states, school boards and individual schools, so students and parents are advised to check their school's official holiday calendar. These breaks also give students opportunities to participate in cultural celebrations and spend time with their families.
